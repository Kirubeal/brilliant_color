How it works

  1. You choose an order quantity: 100, 200 or 300 cases. You pay the cost for every case ordered.
  2. Demand is uncertain: it will be 100, 200 or 300 cases, with the probabilities you entered.
  3. You can only sell what you have and what customers want, so sales = min(order, demand). Unsold cases are lost.
  4. Profit in one scenario: price × min(order, demand) − cost × order.
  5. Expected profit for an order quantity is the probability-weighted average of its three scenario profits: Σ probability × profit.
  6. Recommendation: the order quantity with the highest expected profit.

This is a decision tree: one decision branch per order quantity, then one chance branch per demand level. Expected profit is a long-run average, not a guarantee for any single week.
